US House — NY-10
Lower Manhattan, Park Slope, Borough Park
At Risk
Dan Goldman
Incumbent
Pro-Israel
Strongly pro-Israel. Received $1.7M+ from pro-Israel sources. Endorsed by Hakeem Jeffries, Gov. Hochul.
Endorsed by: Jeffries, Hochul, J Street
Brad Lander
Challenger — Former NYC Comptroller
Anti-Israel
Calls the war "genocide." Attends weekly anti-Israel rallies. Demands AIPAC-free race. Leads in polls 52-33.
Endorsed by: Mayor Mamdani, Jumaane Williams
Why it matters: The marquee pro-Israel vs anti-Israel race in NYC. If Goldman loses, it sends a message that pro-Israel Democrats are vulnerable everywhere. Goldman is outraising Lander ($2.2M vs $750K) but trails in polls.
US House — NY-15
South Bronx, Central Bronx
At Risk
Ritchie Torres
Incumbent
Strongly Pro-Israel
The most pro-Israel Democrat in NYC. Frequent defender of Israel in Congress. Strong AIPAC backing and fundraising.
Endorsed by: AIPAC, pro-Israel PACs
Michael Blake
Challenger — Former Assemblyman, Obama WH alum
Anti-Israel
Making AIPAC donations the centerpiece of his campaign. Launch video directly attacks Torres's Israel support. Lost to Torres in 2020.
Also running: Dalourny Nemorin (DSA organizer)
Why it matters: Torres is the #1 pro-Israel target for the progressive left. A Torres loss would eliminate the most vocal pro-Israel Democrat in NYC. Mamdani's Bronx strength adds progressive energy to challengers.

Washington Heights, Harlem, South Bronx
At Risk
Adriano Espaillat
Incumbent
Pro-Israel
Co-chair of Latino-Jewish Congressional Caucus. $160K+ from AIPAC. Condemned Oct 7. Supports Israel aid.
Endorsed by: AIPAC
Darializa Avila Chevalier
Challenger — Columbia Encampment Organizer
Strongly Anti-Israel
Lead organizer of Columbia pro-Gaza encampment. Supports divestment from Israel. Organized for Mamdani's mayoral campaign.
Endorsed by: NYC-DSA, Justice Democrats
Why it matters: A Chevalier win would flip a pro-Israel seat to a Columbia encampment organizer backed by DSA and Justice Democrats. Espaillat is vulnerable given the Mamdani wave.
